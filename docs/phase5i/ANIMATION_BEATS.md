The original clips are numbered 1–5. Each is 940 × 940 at 24 fps. Clips 1–3 contain 121 frames (5.042 s) and clips 4–5 contain 217 frames (9.042 s). Frame ranges below are inclusive. The media files live in public/images/phase5i.

Scroll coordinates are measured in viewport heights from Home. The action range is the recommended viewing distance. Native playback begins on crossing its leading threshold, with 0.033 viewport heights of directional hysteresis. The reading interval describes the remaining distance before the next beat. If scrolling stops, the action finishes and the still holds indefinitely. At the start, the suit portrait holds from 0.00 to 0.26.

Reverse media starts on the beat’s end frame and plays at positive speed. Each start frame has a matching WebP named by source clip and frame. Within a source, neighbouring actions share a frame. At boundaries between the five original clips, the reverse video finishes on its source first frame and then settles on the preceding clip’s endpoint. These boundaries retain the editorial cuts from the supplied footage.

Home and direct About navigation land on the appropriate still. A fast multi-beat jump or a direction change during playback settles immediately on the latest keyframe once it is decoded. An adjacent move from a settled state plays its cut. A failed or timed-out playback also settles to the appropriate still. There is never a queue of intervening actions.
