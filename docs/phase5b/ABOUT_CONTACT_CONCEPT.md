# About and Contact

## About

The workbench uses a graphite substrate, four terminals, printed traces and one evidence window. The terminal questions are the personal element. Each asks how a piece of work should become clearer, respond to input, be noticed, or change through prototyping.

Design shows the FixLens dashboard. Technology shows Sippy's actual internal assembly. Interaction shows a real gesture test. Making shows Sippy's open shell. Each choice updates the trace, the artifact and its explanation. Project links lead to the full work. The evidence and text are current project material. The page adds no employment, credentials, portrait or biography claim.

The desktop board routes terminals to the central display. The phone board moves the terminals to a connected selection rail above the display. Native buttons and pressed states establish the selection independently of the animation. Later writing can extend the evidence readout without adding another portrait-led section.

## Contact

A line crosses the field from a fixed origin to the GitHub socket. With a mouse, the end of the line follows part of the pointer's movement, making a connection tangible. Near the socket it docks. Keyboard focus docks it too and gives the link a clear focus ring.

The only control is the native GitHub link. Touch and reduced motion present the docked connection. The port carries the name and handle, and stays in a stable position during input. The patch can support additional verified outlets in a future pass.
