# Projects interaction

The visitor enters one continuous floor and sees two names, three authentic product images and two direct case-study links immediately. FixLens starts with more floor space. Sippy is visible from the first view and has a separate, green field.

## FixLens

The interface pair is the installed property-manager dashboard (FX02) and the implemented renter Home presentation (FX03) from the existing asset manifest. The two elements stay on separate display planes. The monitor establishes the system and the higher, angled phone gives the renter a position alongside it. A perspective floor and a shared trace communicate that the two views belong to one maintenance process. The trace is conceptual. It does not show a measured data path.

Manager and Renter are native buttons with a pressed state. A role choice shifts the foreground plane and updates the short description. A mouse can also shift the two image planes in opposite directions by a few pixels. The selected perspective and actual case-study link work by keyboard and tap.

## Sippy

The approved plush cutout appears without a screen frame. Concentric floor marks and a soft shadow give it a point of contact. The scroll passage gives Sippy more room and brings the cutout toward the centre. Come closer and Step back move the approved photo inside the exhibition. The original product form and expression stay intact.

## Passage between rooms

At 901 px and above, with at least 640 px of height and motion allowed, a short native sticky track moves the seam from 71% to 28%. The stage has one region whose width changes for both projects. Scrolling back restores the same geometry. The two numbered view controls move to either endpoint with a normal scroll. The case-study links above the floor go directly to FixLens and Sippy independently of the selected room. Each room provides its own link when the opening links leave view.

On phones the two rooms stack in the natural page flow. At medium widths and under reduced motion a stable composition keeps both projects available. Role selection and the object control remain functional without the passage animation. WillDo follows the exhibition as a smaller future-work entry with its verified development status.
